  Bloodstained, ドラクエ, ロマサガ, Atlus Sakura Wars sprite work.

- Russian / Eastern European: Russian pixel indies and studios (e.g. ZA/UM-adjacent artists,
  Ice-Pick Lodge, Pixel Crushers, Russian Habr postmortems of pixel games, "Loop Hero",
  "Black Book", "Atomicrops"-style), plus Russian translations/analyses of all of the above.
