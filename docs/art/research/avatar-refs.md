# Avatar reference library (links)

The CocoPPa Play / SELFY-style reference images gathered for hair, outfit and avatar work, sorted by
kind. The images are not kept in the repo; re-download them from these links into a scratch folder
and make contact sheets to study them. What they teach is written up in [avatar.md](avatar.md).

Sources:
- Pinterest public "ideas" pages: https://www.pinterest.com/ideas/cocoppa-play-hair/944781960173/ and
  https://www.pinterest.com/ideas/cocoppaplay-outfits/909058991985/ (mostly official item renders, with
  some fan art and tutorials).
- The CoCoppa event and gacha archive feed: https://cocoppa-items.tumblr.com/rss (official item art).
- A player’s gallery of finished looks: https://antiamorous.neocities.org/cocoppa.
- The official X account, posts 828484612334587905 (Cat Hair) and 829239875580866562 (Candy Hair & Eyes),
  fetched through api.fxtwitter.com.
