# Research: Dancing Green (FFXIV boss reference)

The player wants a boss modelled on Dancing Green, the first boss of FFXIV's AAC Cruiserweight
tier (M5 / M5S, patch 7.2), built on the hero body. Sources are listed at the end.

## Who he is and how he looks
- **Role:** a male Rava Viera, "Party Animal", self-styled king of the dance floor and a "shameless
  playboy". He fights with the soul of a toad (a Feral Soul) and has frog backup dancers.
- **Look:** long upright rabbit ears furred in his hair colour, with pink inside. Messy
  shoulder-length hair, lime to yellow, greener at the tips. Tan skin. Big pink cat-eye
  sunglasses, and a round badge pinned in the hair. A shiny purple jacket worn open with the
  sleeves rolled, with a patterned band across the chest. A lime or yellow patterned shirt with
  a tassel. White bell-bottom flares and white platform shoes.
- **Fan chibi art** keeps the yellow-to-green hair, the pink shades, the purple jacket and the
  white flares. The pose is relaxed: finger guns, a wink and a sparkle.

## How he moves (studied from the clips)
- **Grounded and upright.** Big body tilts, spins and leaps are wrong for him: the style is in
  the limbs, on the beat, with holds.
- **The point:** the signature pose. One finger straight up, the other hand on the hip, the hip
  pushed out and weight on one leg. He holds it with small bobs, sweeps the arm down across the
  body, and points again.
- **The groove:** the knees bounce on every beat, the hips sway a little, and the head bobs a
  beat late.
- **Steps:** side step-touches with fists swinging, and a straight-leg kick forward.
- **The strut:** easy steps with the arms swinging, leaning back a touch.
- **Flirty beats:** finger guns with a wink, and a blown kiss that sends off a pink heart.
- **Moonwalk:** his frog dancers moonwalk across the floor and leave tracks that explode.
- **Savage intro:** a red flash of the toad's soul around him.

## From the full fight video (the player's upload, M5 on Dawntrail)
- **Intro (0–53 s):**
  - A close-up of platform shoes stepping in, then a cocky dance on the stage in front of a neon
    sign with disco balls: a knee-up kick, a lean back with the arms out, and a straight-leg kick.
  - The hero walks onto the 8x8 glowing tile floor.
  - He strikes the point pose and a close-up shows the shades and pin. Red energy builds around
    him and swallows him.
  - **He transforms into his toad form**, the shape he fights in. Shown in order: orange frog
    feet with big round toes under chrome flares; a frog hand covered in rings holding a vinyl
    record; then the head.
  - He ends on a pose: one hand behind the head, the other arm pointing the record straight out.
- **The toad form (how he looks for the whole fight):**
  - **Head:** a tall frog head, lime green, still with the rabbit ears, and big pink goggle eyes
    like a visor. A thick red scarf ruff at the neck.
  - **Upper body:** a cropped, shiny purple jacket with rolled sleeves over a silver ribbed chest.
  - **Waist and legs:** a white belt with a round buckle, and chrome or silver bell-bottoms
    sculpted like armour panels.
  - **Hands and feet:** big orange frog hands with rings, and orange frog feet.
  - **Tail:** a long red curling tail (or ribbon) that whips around him all fight. It's the
    liveliest part of his silhouette.
  - **Scale:** about twice the hero's height.
- **How he moves in the fight:** he stays at the middle of the floor and snaps from pose to
  pose on the music. Typical poses:
  - leaning with both arms thrown wide (a T), one knee up;
  - a deep side lunge with one arm pointing;
  - hip twists with his arms out.

  The red tail swings in big S-curves behind every move. On the Twists, a stepped wall of
  glowing white cubes builds up on one side and that side flashes, beat by beat. For Do the
  Hustle he poses toward the side that gets hit.
- **Floor and effects seen:**
  - Funky Floor turns the tiles into a red-orange and black checkerboard, and then into a
    rainbow of glowing tiles.
  - Disco Infernal drops white spotlight cylinders that drift around.
  - The frog dancers stand on pedestals along the back wall, tethered to him by blue lightning.
  - "PERFECT GROOVE" flashes in big neon letters when the party dodges everything.
  - Frogtourage frogs spin out as star-shaped dancers.

## What he does in the fight (ability order and tells)
- **Do the Hustle:** a wall of rainbow tiles lights up one side, then a half-room cleave there.
  Move to the other half.
- **2-snap and 4-snap Twist (with Drop the Needle in Savage):** a side lights up and is hit two
  or four times on the beat, then the opposite side is hit with no warning. Hold, then cross.
- **Deep Cut:** a cone tankbuster on the two tanks.
- **Flip to A-side or B-side (Savage):** stores an attack that the next Twist releases. A-side
  is a protean cone on one tank, one healer and one DPS. B-side is a line stack on the healers.
- **Funky Floor:** the 8x8 floor flashes in a checkerboard five times; Full Beat stacks on one
  player during it.
- **Disco Infernal:** a raid-wide hit plus "Burn Baby Burn" timers, with spotlights moving over
  the floor. When your timer ends you must be standing in a spotlight, alone, to pose. Missing
  one stuns you and hits hard (in Savage it kills).
- **Celebrate Good Times:** a plain raid-wide hit.
- **Ride the Waves:** lines of tiles sweep down from the north, each with a gap to stand in.
- **Ensemble Assemble, then Arcady Night Fever, then Let's Dance!:** he moves north and calls
  eight frog dancers on pedestals. They pose one by one, raising a left or right hand. He tethers
  them in order and does eight half-room cleaves, each following a dancer's raised hand. Dodging
  fills a "Grooviness" gauge.
- **Let's Pose!:** a raid-wide hit that is weaker the more you dodged; a perfect run gives a
  damage buff (and a Limit Break bar if the whole party was perfect).
- **Frogtourage:** frogs moonwalk across the floor, and their tracks explode (Moonburn).
  Eighth Beats spreads everyone and Quarter Beats stacks pairs.
- **Arcady Night Encore and Let's Dance! Remix:** the dance again, with the frogs facing any way.
- **Enrage:** two Celebrate Good Times in a row, Frogtourage Finale (the whole frog army), then
  Hi-NRG Fever, which wipes the party.

## For Tengu
- Build him on the hero body: bunny ears, the disco outfit, big shades.
- Keep the dance grounded: snap into each pose on the beat (about a tenth of a second) and hold,
  with the knee groove underneath. No spins or leaps unless an attack calls for one.
- His attacks map onto our side view: Do the Hustle and the Twists become left or right
  half-screen cleaves on the beat, telegraphed by a glowing wall of rainbow light on that side.
  Funky Floor becomes checkerboard floor tiles. Disco Infernal becomes spotlights to stand in.
  Let's Dance becomes the frog line pointing left or right. Frogtourage becomes frogs
  moonwalking across the floor.
