# Research: Diives (safe-for-work animation only)

Artist: Diives (DiivesArt). They are best known for cartoon character animation. A lot of their
work is adult or heavy fan service. Only clearly safe-for-work clips are kept here, each one
checked by eye. Collected from Tenor's search pages for "diives", "diivesart" and "diives-art".

**Left out on purpose:**
- the adult and suggestive clips (dancing and shaking, underwear and topless poses, chest
  close-ups);
- meme clips that only use the name.

Diives is **not pixel art**. It is smooth vector animation at about 24 fps. Use it for character
acting, expressions and design, not for pixel technique (see [sakuemon.md](sakuemon.md) for that).

## What they teach

1. **The face does the acting.**
   - Huge eyes with a big white and a large flat iris, and one or two white catchlights.
   - Brows are small and very mobile.
   - Mouths change shape a lot: a tiny dot, a wide grin, a wobbly worried line, an open shout
     with a tongue.
   - Blush is a strong patch of hatched lines across the nose and cheeks.
2. **Emotion symbols do half the work:** hearts around the head and heart pupils, sweat drops,
   exclamation marks, steam puffs, sparkles, motion lines, thought clouds, and speech bubbles
   that pop in with a squash.
3. **Squash and stretch on the whole head.** A startle stretches the head up, then snaps back.
   Joy squashes it with closed eyes. Shy acting hides the face behind the hands or hair.
4. **Timing.** Holds broken by snappy changes. A pose holds, a quick 2–3 frame move reaches the
   next pose with overshoot, then it holds again. Small idle bobs and blinks keep the holds alive.
5. **Design and rendering.**
   - Big, simple hair masses with a few spikes and loops.
   - Thick, clean outlines in a dark version of each colour (deep red-brown, not black).
   - Flat colours, with one shadow tone at most.
   - Large heads on small bodies for gags; mostly bust shots for acting.

## Apply to Tengu

- **Expressions** (the face sets in ): stronger mouth shapes, hatched blush, heart
  pupils, popping symbols with a squash, and a head squash and stretch on startle and joy.
- **Emotes and chat bubbles:** pop in with an overshoot, then hold.
- **NPC dialog portraits:** acting with holds and quick changes; shy and nervous fidgets.
