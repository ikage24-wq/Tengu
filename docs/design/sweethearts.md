# Sweethearts

Built 2026-11-04 (the player asked for a dating system with new townsfolk: date them, gift them,
play with them, live with them; and picked: six of them, friendship or romance, full home life,
dates as shared activities, the name "Sweethearts"). Pictures: `docs/design/samples/`.

## The six

| Who | Where | Spot | Loves a... |
|---|---|---|---|
| Poppy, sunny baker | Sprout Village, Windmill Square | her bakery cart | cooking date |
| Lark, shy lighthouse poet | Lantern Lagoon, Shell Cove | an upturned rowboat and a crate desk | night of stargazing |
| Rowan, gentle giant knight | Ember Crag, Arena Plateau | his weapon rack, a helmet of daisies | ride together |
| Yuzu, playful fox spirit | Moonleaf Grove, by the Moon Tree | a little red shrine with a fox statue | lantern dance |
| Celeste, sleepy stargazer | Moonleaf Grove, Rune Garden | a telescope, a blanket, star charts | night of stargazing |
| Marlowe, cheeky young sailor | Pastel Harbor, Fish Market | his patched boat up on blocks | fishing date |

Each has their own outfit (never sold), idle habits with props (Poppy kneads and tastes, Lark
writes and hides in her scarf, Rowan polishes his sword and sniffs daisies, Yuzu pounces and chases
her tail, Celeste dozes with z's and counts stars, Marlowe uses his spyglass and ties knots), their
own loves, likes and dislikes, talk that changes as you grow closer, and seven heart scenes.

## Hearts

- Ten hearts (100 points each). **Chat** once a day (+20). **One gift a day**: loved +80, liked
  +45, anything else +20, disliked -20. The gift page remembers how they took each thing.
- **Heart scenes** at 2, 4 and 6 hearts, then 8 and 10 as friends, or their romance versions as
  sweethearts.
- **Friendship or romance:** you can be best friends with all six. A **Promise Bouquet** (crafted,
  Gifts tab) at 6 hearts asks one to be your sweetheart. One sweetheart at a time. Saying goodbye
  is gentle: hearts dip a little, and another bouquet makes up.
- The **Sweethearts** tab in the Collection (B) shows everyone's hearts, where they are, the gifts
  you've found they love, and your dates.

## Dates (shared activities, one a day each)

From 3 hearts, **Ask on a date**. They come along at your side wherever you are.

- **Fishing date:** catch 3 fish together; rare ones count double.
- **Ride together:** ride your mount a good way; they ride a Puff Bun beside you; jumping together
  counts.
- **Stargazing:** press Space as each shooting star crosses the middle of the sky to wish on it.
- **Cooking date:** cook a dish together at a town stove; one they love makes it a great date.
- **Lantern dance:** press the arrow that comes round, on the beat.

How it went (great, good, okay) and whether it's their favourite kind decide the hearts (45 to
150). Every date has a time limit, and even a clumsy one is a nice time.

## Living together

At 10 hearts as sweethearts, a **Seashell Ring** (crafted) asks them to move in. You need your
island first.

- They leave their town spot and live on your island, each at their own place.
- They bring a piece of their own into the hut (Poppy's cake, Lark's net, Rowan's moon lamp, Yuzu's
  stool, Celeste's globe, Marlowe's plaque); it goes back with them if you part.
- Coming home, they welcome you, and once a day they've cooked you something.
- **Come adventuring:** your sweetheart joins your party in their own job (Rowan a Warrior,
  Celeste a Star Witch, Lark and Marlowe Archers, Poppy and Yuzu Wind Dancers) and fights beside you.
- Friends visiting your island online see who lives with you.
