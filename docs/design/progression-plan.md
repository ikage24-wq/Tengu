# Progression plan: giving the game a meaning

Goal: a reason to play beyond "numbers go up". The model is **MapleStory** (job advancement,
grinding maps, bosses with weekly rewards, collecting, many characters that help each other) mixed
with **FFXIV** (a main story that carries you through the world and unlocks it, dungeons and boss
trials as story beats, roles, gear from duties, side content).

"Continue the progression plan" means: read this file, pick the next unchecked phase item, build
it, and tick it off here.

## Where the game stands (Oct 2026)

- Levels 1–100 and gear every 5 levels up to 100, but maps and monsters only go up to about 50.
- Quests: 9, loose, no story thread. Every map is open from the start.
- Jobs: Warrior, Archer and Thief get a first job at 10 and two paths at 30 after a trial. The
  Mage was removed; a new magic class is being built from scratch.
- Group content: Rootwood Hollow (Lv 10, tank/healer/2 DPS, three bosses) and the Crystal King
  (Dancing Green and Roxy's Boss Rush were a test boss, removed 2026-10-29).
- Drawn but unused: Sylph, Nekomata, Kitsune Miko, Lupina, Lamia, Yuki-onna, and four queens
  (Sylvana Lv 22, Tamamo Lv 30, Lilith Lv 38, Selene Lv 50). These make ready-made story bosses.
- Cash flows freely (5,000 to start, more every level), so outfits don't say anything about you.

## The core loop we want

1. **The story** pulls you to the next region and opens it up.
2. A region gives you **maps to hunt**, its **side quests**, and its **collection page**.
3. Each story chapter ends in a **dungeon or a boss trial** (FFXIV style), and its rewards are
   the gear you need for the next chapter.
4. **Job advancements** are the big milestones (at 30, 60 and 100, done as trial quests).
5. Endgame and between chapters: **weekly bosses**, **daily tasks**, **Boss Rush**, collecting,
   titles, outfits you earn, and **raising more characters, which helps every character**.

## Pillars

### 1. Main story (FFXIV's main story quest)
A chain of story quests marked with a special icon, split into chapters by level. **Optional** (the
player's call, 2026-11-01): every region is open from the start; only the story's own arenas wait for it.
(Was: regions stay
**locked until the story reaches them** (a collapsed bridge, a sealed ruin gate, a wind wall
around the Sky Garden), so the world opens up as you go. Each chapter gets a cutscene system
(letterbox shots, name cards) for its big moments.

Premise (draft): Tengu Island's guardian spirit, the Tengu, has gone silent, and the island's
spirits are souring: the old trees grow grumpy, the fox spirits play cruel tricks, and the crystals
are turning on the people who mine them. Mira takes you on as a newcomer and you travel the island
to calm each region's spirit, find out who is souring them, and wake the Tengu.

### 2. Job advancement for everyone (MapleStory)
- Every class advances: **1st at 10, 2nd at 30, 3rd at 60, 4th at 100** (the new magic class
  too, once it's built).
- Each advancement is a short trial quest from the instructor: go somewhere, prove yourself in a
  solo trial room, come back to a ceremony with a new look (aura, title, outfit piece).
- Warrior 2nd jobs are in (Paladin tank, Berserker damage), Archer 2nd jobs (Beastmaster and
  Stormcaller, both damage) and Thief 2nd jobs (Shinobi and Kitsune, both damage); the new
  magic class next.
- Role stays tied to job like FFXIV: warriors tank, the new magic class will heal, archers and
  thieves deal damage (until then an archer or thief fills the healer's seat).
  The 2nd job can add a second role (for example a warrior branch that deals damage).

### 3. Many characters, one account (MapleStory's Legion)
The character tree already holds 8 heroes. Each hero you raise adds a **bonus for the whole
account** (a Warrior at 30 gives everyone a bit more max HP, a Thief at 60 a bit more crit, and so
on), shown as a board you fill in. This makes playing every job worth it.

(Alternative, FFXIV style: one hero can learn every job and switch by changing weapon. Not
recommended: it fights the 8-slot character tree, the job-town identity, and how gear is tied to
jobs.)

### 4. Dungeons and trials as story beats (FFXIV duties)
- Each chapter ends in a dungeon (four players, tank/healer/2 DPS, like Rootwood Hollow) or a
  **trial** (one big boss arena with telegraphed moves on a rhythm).
- The group finder already exists; add a **daily bonus** for the first run of the day (FFXIV
  roulette) so old dungeons stay useful and new players always find a group.
- Dungeons drop **tokens** you trade for that chapter's gear set, so bad luck still makes progress.

### 5. Gear progression
- Story dungeons give the "next step" gear; fields and shops fill gaps.
- Charm slots were removed (2026-10-31): polishing at Tora's is the one way to strengthen a piece.
  Any later upgrade system replaces polishing rather than stacking on top of it, and **never
  destroys items**, to keep it cozy.
- Endgame designs (Holy, Demonic, Bone, Laser, Chainsaw, Cyber) move to boss and weekly rewards,
  so they mean "I beat that".

### 6. Bosses and weekly goals (MapleStory bossing)
- Every boss gets **Normal** and **Hard**. Hard drops the best stuff and a boss trophy.
- **Weekly clears**: each boss gives its big reward once a week (per character), so there's a
  weekly routine without endless grinding.
- **Boss Rush** (to be rebuilt) gets grades (S/A/B), best times, and a trophy wall.

### 7. Daily and weekly tasks
- **Daily tasks** from each town's board: "hunt 100 Wargs", "deliver 20 Dryad Leaves". These pay EXP,
  gold, and a currency for that town's shop (recipes, outfits, mounts).
- **Weekly**: weekly bosses, a weekly dungeon goal.
- Kept short: a full daily round should take about 15 minutes.

### 8. Collecting
- **Monster book** (MapleStory monster book plus FFXIV hunting log): a page per monster that fills
  in (seen → its drops found → mastered after N kills). Finishing pages gives small permanent
  bonuses and stickers on the page.
- **Achievements and titles**: shown under your name. "Slime Slayer", "Untouchable" (beat a boss
  without being hit), "Fashionista", one per story chapter, one per boss on Hard.
- **Earned outfits**: lower the free Cash and put the best outfits behind achievements, boss
  trophies, and town currencies. The Cash Shop stays, but the special looks are earned.

### 9. Life and fun (later)
- **Fun Fair** (FFXIV's Gold Saucer): Boss Rush, minigames, its own prize tokens and outfits.
- **Mounts**: from story chapters and achievements.
- **A cozy home** (FFXIV housing) to show off trophies and furniture. Much later.
- **Life skills (fishing, mining, gathering, crafting)**: important to the player (2026-11-01), not "much later": every map gets spots for them that fit its theme and zones.
