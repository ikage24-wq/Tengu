# Your island

Built 2026-11-04 (the player picked: a tropical beach cove, a Home Shell plus a slow boat ride,
five stages, a hermit crab carpenter, decorating inside and out). Research:
`docs/art/research/home-mounts.md`. Pictures: `docs/design/samples/island/`.

## Getting it and getting there

- **Captain Rook's quest "A Deed to a Little Island"** (Pastel Harbor, Lv 10) gives you the island
  and a **Home Shell**.
- **The Home Shell** takes you home from anywhere outside a dungeon, trial or voyage. Used on the
  island, it takes you back to where you were. It is never used up.
- **The slow way:** Captain Bobbin at the harbor quay sails you out on the Sea Biscuit (about half
  a minute, and you can fish off the stern). From the island, your own skiff at the dock (Up)
  sails you back to the harbor.
- The island is yours alone. No bots or passers-by come there.

## Shelly and the five stages

Shelly is a hermit crab carpenter with a big pink spiral shell on her back and coral claw
mittens. She taps away with her mallet, measures with her claws and tucks shyly into her shell.
She builds the next stage when you bring what she needs. Every stage adds something to do:

| Stage | Adds | Shelly needs |
|---|---|---|
| 1 Shell Beach | The dock and skiff, the beach, Shelly's conch workshop, your hut on stilts, a garden plot (Lucky Clover), a bouncy clam up to the hut's roof deck, sea fishing off the far end | (free with the deed) |
| 2 Tide Pond | A pond behind the hut with its own fish, a fishing jetty, reeds | 3,000 gold, 10 Oak Logs, 6 Cotton, 6 Mossy Scutes |
| 3 The Rocky Rise | Mossy boulders, an ore rock, a herb patch | 8,000 gold, 12 Copper Ore, 8 Maple Logs, 10 Lucky Clover |
| 4 Coral Islet | A rope bridge over the shallows to a second islet, a cherry tree and a cotton patch on its beach, a cliff (up a ladder) with a little shack and a hammock | 20,000 gold, 20 Maple Logs, 10 Iron Ore, 8 Silkmoss, 6 Hollow Acorns |
| 5 Sunset Terrace | A boardwalk and railing along the cliff top, bunting and a lantern: the place for your trophies | 50,000 gold, 15 Cedar Logs, 10 Silver Ore, 8 Lotus Fibre |

The hut grows with the island: a window box (2), a porch rail and shell chime (3), a second
window and a welcome mat (4), a star on the roof (5).

The island's spots are small and refill like any map's, so it adds to hunting rather than
replacing it. Nothing withers and there is no upkeep.

## The hut inside

One cosy room: plank walls, beams, a big round window onto the sea, a mint door, a rug and two
wall shelves to put things on.

## Furniture and decorating (just for looks: the player's choice)

- **Crafted** at any bench, in the new **Home** tab, from what you gather, mine, fish and hunt.
  Recipes need an island; the bakery set opens with the Tide Pond (stage 2), Starry Night with the
  Rocky Rise (3), the crown cushion with Coral Islet (4).
- **23 pieces in four sets:** Seaside Cottage (driftwood table and chair, wave quilt bed, shell
  lamp, rope hammock, fishing net), Sweet Bakery (cake stand table, macaron stool, cupcake lamp,
  strawberry rug, bakery shelf, cake under glass), Starry Night (crescent moon bed, star string
  lights, brass telescope that turns, spinning planet globe, moon floor lamp, star clock that
  keeps the game's time), Trophies and Catches (record catch plaque showing your biggest fish,
  mount figurine of the mount you ride, dungeon cup with your best grade, crown cushion that gets a
  crown once you clear a dungeon on Hard, derby ribbon with your Fishing Derby wins).
- **Decorate mode** (Y, or the Decorate button at home): your furniture in a bar at the bottom,
  by set. Pick a piece and a see-through preview follows the pointer, snapped to where it would
  go: soft green where it fits, soft red with a reason where it doesn't ("Put it on a table or a
  shelf", "It bumps into the Shell Lamp", "It would get wet!") and the blocking piece outlined.
  Click to set it down (it hops and settles with a wooden knock); click a placed piece to move
  it; F or right-click flips; Delete puts it away for free; Ctrl+Z / Ctrl+Y undo and redo; Shift
  places off the grid. You can walk about while decorating.
- **Where things go:** floor pieces on the ground or a ledge (never in the water), rugs on the
  floor, small pieces on tables and shelves, wall pieces inside on the back wall, string lights
  from the ceiling beam or under a deck. Some pieces are inside-only (beds, wall pieces) or
  outside-only (the hammock). A table with something on it has to be cleared before it moves.
  Up to 60 pieces on the island and 60 in the hut.

## Visiting friends

Online, right-click a friend and **Visit their island**: you arrive at their dock and see their
island at its own stage with their furniture and their records on the trophies. Their game tells
them you've come. Only people on the same island see each other there. You can't move their
furniture. Your Home Shell or their skiff takes you back.

## Water

The pond, the shallows and the sea are real water: you wade slowly through it, or skim across
on the Shell Skipper.
