# Stormwall

The Thunderguard's tank path at level 30 (2026-11-09), after Brann's trial. The storm as a
fortress: where the Tempest is the bolt, the Stormwall is the thunderhead, huge, slow and
unmovable, with the lightning inside it. He replaces the Paladin as the Warrior line's tank path.
Held to the Tide Mage's standard. (To be built after the Tempest.)

## Who he is

He stands like a mountain with a storm on its peak: deep stances, heavy deliberate blows, every one
a rumble of thunder. He doesn't chase; he pulls monsters in to him and holds them there. A
thundercloud gathers over him as he fights and answers anything that hits his friends.

- **Colour:** deep storm indigo and slate, rain silver, white-gold lightning (the Thunderguard's gold,
  heavier and slower), cloud grey.
- **Shapes, his alone:** thunderheads and rolling cloud, curtains of rain, rings of lightning pillars
  (a cage), plates of crackling static that lock together into a shield, magnetic field lines that
  drag, and the line's crescent slash in indigo and gold.
- **Feel:** weight. Long, braced wind-ups, a planted strike that lands like a falling wall, a
  ground-shaking settle. He barely steps; the monsters come to him.

## Basic attacks

Four steps, each from a pool of three: a braced overhead, a shoulder-forward shove behind a plate of
static, a pull (a cut that drags them in on field lines); a heavy return, a stamp that rolls thunder
along the floor, a rising guard-cut; a wide cleave under a falling curtain of rain, a lightning cage
snapped round the nearest few, a hammering double; finisher: the thunderhead's bolt down on the
pack, a slam that throws up a wall of rain and lightning, a whirling sweep that drags everything in.

## Thunderhead (his gauge)

Built by the hits he takes and by holding monsters on him; slow to fade. The fuller it runs the
less he takes. At half, a thundercloud gathers over him and drops a bolt on whatever hits him or his
party. Full (Eye of the Storm): every monster near him is held to him, the cloud's bolts fall on
anything that attacks the party, and Fortress is ready.

## Skills

His own: **Lightning Cage** (pillars of lightning snap up in a ring: every monster inside is pulled
in, taunted and dazed), **Storm Wall** (a wall of rain and lightning in front of the party that
takes the edge off everything that comes through it), **Magnetize** (field lines drag a group to
him), **Overcast** (party barrier: a cloud over everyone that soaks up hits), **Bedrock** (passive:
toughness), **Fortress** (finisher: a thunderhead over the whole fight, a ring of lightning pillars
round the party, everyone shielded, every monster taunted and struck). And the Thunderguard's seven
played his own way.
