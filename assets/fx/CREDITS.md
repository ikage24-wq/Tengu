# Effect art credits

The effect sprite strips in this folder are cut from these packs by `tools/build-fx.py`, some
recoloured (a hue shift that keeps the artist's shading). All three packs are public domain (CC0);
credit is given with thanks.

- **PVFX Foundry** by Pixel VFX Studio (nerijs), CC0 1.0. "VFX from PVFX Foundry by Pixel VFX
  Studio." https://nerijs.itch.io/pvfx-foundry
  Used: leaf-gust, splash-crown, magical-projectile, crescent-slash, landing-dust, smoke-puff,
  beam-cutoff-burst, electric-impact, focus-charge, lattice-beam, solar-shrapnel, frost-nova,
  warm-explosion, resonant-break, ember-jet, radiant-heal, arcane-parry, mass-release,
  earth-rupture, acid-splash, cinder-orchid.
- **Free Pixel Effects Pack** by CodeManu and Davit Masia, CC0 (public domain).
  https://opengameart.org/content/free-pixel-effects-pack
  Used: firespin (recoloured to wind and steel), freezing, nebula, sunburn, phantom (recoloured
  rose), weaponhit, flamelash, vortex (recoloured blood red).
- **Pixel art sword slash effect** by tbbk, CC0 (public domain).
  https://opengameart.org/content/pixel-art-sword-slash-effect
  Used: slash (the cut mark where a blade lands).

Only packs whose license allows shipping their files in the game's own files belong here (CC0 or
CC BY, with credit). Packs that forbid redistributing the files can't be used, even if free.
