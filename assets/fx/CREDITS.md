# Effect art credits

Most of the game's effects are Tengu's own art (`tools/fx-art.js`, the `cel*` strips), drawn for
the game and matched to its sprites. The archer paths' effects are still cut from these packs by
`tools/build-fx.py`, some recoloured (a hue shift that keeps the artist's shading). Both packs are
public domain (CC0); credit is given with thanks.

- **PVFX Foundry** by Pixel VFX Studio (nerijs), CC0 1.0. "VFX from PVFX Foundry by Pixel VFX
  Studio." https://nerijs.itch.io/pvfx-foundry
  Used: leaf-gust, splash-crown, magical-projectile, crescent-slash, landing-dust, smoke-puff,
  beam-cutoff-burst, electric-impact, focus-charge, solar-shrapnel, frost-nova, warm-explosion,
  resonant-break.
- **Free Pixel Effects Pack** by CodeManu and Davit Masia, CC0 (public domain).
  https://opengameart.org/content/free-pixel-effects-pack
  Used: firespin (recoloured to wind), freezing, nebula, sunburn, phantom (recoloured rose),
  weaponhit.

Only packs whose license allows shipping their files in the game's own files belong here (CC0 or
CC BY, with credit). Packs that forbid redistributing the files can't be used, even if free.
