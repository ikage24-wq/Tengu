# Effect art credits

The effect sprite strips in this folder are cut from these packs by `tools/build-fx.py`, some
recoloured (a hue shift that keeps the artist's shading, or a white texture coloured like light).
Every pack is public domain (CC0) or CC BY (credited as its license asks); credit is given
with thanks.

HD effect art (what the game's effects are built from):

- **Sprite Effects, parts 1 and 2** by jellyfish0, CC0.
  https://opengameart.org/content/sprite-effects-part-1 and
  https://opengameart.org/content/sprite-effects-part-2
  Used: the painted sword swipes (50h, 50f, 50e recoloured blood red), the spin slash (50g),
  claw slashes (50i, recoloured red), the gold crescent burst (29), the pillar of light (3,
  coloured gold, steel and red), the golden magic circle (40a) and the orb shield (62, recoloured
  gold).
- **2DFX Impact Spritesheets** by Inguz Media, CC BY 4.0.
  https://opengameart.org/content/2dfx-impact-spritesheets
  Used: impact04 (the cyan blade-hit flash).
- **Slash** (hit spark) by Raja_Kutai, CC0. https://opengameart.org/content/slash-0
  Used: the anime star burst for critical hits.
- **Auras** by Pavel Kutejnikov, CC0. https://opengameart.org/content/auras
  Used: the ring of fire (001_fire) under a raging Berserker.
- **game-icons.net**, CC BY 3.0 (https://game-icons.net). Shapes rendered and turned into
  glowing effect art (`tools/build-fx.py`): by **Lorc** (http://lorcblog.blogspot.com):
  crossed-slashes, spiky-explosion, whirlwind, lion, magic-shield, crossed-swords, holy-symbol,
  energy-shield, winged-sword, angel-wings, horned-skull; by **Caro Asercion**: heraldic-sun; by
  **Sbed** (http://opengameart.org/content/95-game-icons): evil-wings.
- **Brackeys VFX Bundle** (Brackeys), CC0. https://brackeysgames.itch.io/brackeys-vfx-bundle
  Particle textures by **Kenney** (www.kenney.nl) and Picster, flipbooks by **Thomas Iché**.
  Used: spotlight, effect (rays), circle, trace, twirl and dirt textures; the explosion smoke,
  flame and fire flipbooks.
- **Sinestesia**, CC0, on OpenGameArt: "Animations - Blood, Hit and Both"
  (https://opengameart.org/node/81933), "Hit Animation" (https://opengameart.org/node/80085) and
  "2D Explosion Animations #2" (https://opengameart.org/node/74923).
- **Weapon Slash - Effect** by Cethiel, CC0.
  https://opengameart.org/content/weapon-slash-effect
  Used: the thrust, steel blue and recoloured red.

Only packs whose license allows shipping their files in the game's own files belong here (CC0 or
CC BY, with credit). Packs that forbid redistributing the files can't be used, even if free.
