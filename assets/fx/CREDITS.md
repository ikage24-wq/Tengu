# Effect art credits

The effect sprite strips in this folder are cut from these packs by `tools/build-fx.py`, some
recoloured (a hue shift that keeps the artist's shading, or a white texture coloured like light).
Every pack is public domain (CC0); credit is given with thanks.

HD effect art (what the game's effects are built from):

- **Brackeys VFX Bundle** (Brackeys), CC0. https://brackeysgames.itch.io/brackeys-vfx-bundle
  Particle textures by **Kenney** (www.kenney.nl) and Picster, flipbooks by **Thomas Iché**.
  Used: spotlight, star, effect (rays), circle, magic, trace, twirl, slash and dirt textures;
  the explosion smoke, wispy smoke, flame and fire flipbooks.
- **Sinestesia**, CC0, on OpenGameArt: "Animations - Blood, Hit and Both"
  (https://opengameart.org/node/81933), "Hit Animation" (https://opengameart.org/node/80085),
  "Hit Animation 2" (https://opengameart.org/node/84746) and "2D Explosion Animations #2"
  (https://opengameart.org/node/74923).
- **Weapon Slash - Effect** by Cethiel, CC0.
  https://opengameart.org/content/weapon-slash-effect
  Used: the thrust and the slash line (cut marks), steel blue and recoloured red.

Pixel effect art (kept only until the archer paths are redone in HD):

- **PVFX Foundry** by Pixel VFX Studio (nerijs), CC0 1.0. "VFX from PVFX Foundry by Pixel VFX
  Studio." https://nerijs.itch.io/pvfx-foundry
  Used: leaf-gust, splash-crown, magical-projectile, crescent-slash, landing-dust, smoke-puff,
  beam-cutoff-burst, electric-impact, focus-charge, solar-shrapnel, frost-nova, warm-explosion,
  resonant-break.
- **Free Pixel Effects Pack** by CodeManu and Davit Masia, CC0 (public domain).
  https://opengameart.org/content/free-pixel-effects-pack
  Used: firespin (recoloured to wind), freezing, nebula, sunburn, phantom (recoloured rose),
  weaponhit.

Only packs whose license allows shipping their files in the game's own files belong here (CC0 or
CC BY, with credit). Packs that forbid redistributing the files can't be used, even if free.
