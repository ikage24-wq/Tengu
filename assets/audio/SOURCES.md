# Sound sources

The music and most sound effects are made in code. The few recordings in `sfx.mp3` are public
domain (CC0), so nothing needs crediting: Kenney's RPG Audio, Impact Sounds and Interface Sounds
packs, and CC0 packs from OpenGameArt (artisticdude, Ogrebane, rubberduck, Still North Media,
qubodup). Only add recordings that are CC0. Rebuild with `node tools/build-audio.mjs`.
