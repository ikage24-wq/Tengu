# Sound credits

- **Instrument recordings**: from the FluidR3 GM soundfont by Frank Wen, used under
  [Creative Commons Attribution 3.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/3.0/), via
  [gleitz/midi-js-soundfonts](https://github.com/gleitz/midi-js-soundfonts). Notes were trimmed and
  packed into one file per instrument (`inst/`).
- **Sound effect recordings**: from Kenney's *RPG Audio*, *Impact Sounds* and *Interface Sounds*
  packs ([kenney.nl](https://kenney.nl)), public domain (CC0). Trimmed and packed into `sfx.mp3`.
- **Combat recordings** (weapon swings, clangs, bows and arrows, spells, monsters), trimmed and
  packed into `sfx.mp3`:
  - [Fantasy SFX Pack Vol 1](https://opengameart.org/content/jc-sounds-fantasy-sfx-pack-vol-1) by
    **JC Sounds**, [CC BY 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/): sword, heavy sword
    and dagger swings and hits, bow draw, shot and hit, fireball, ice shard and electric spells,
    healing chime, magic shield, teleport.
  - [Magic Spell SFX Soundpack](https://thesoundrack.itch.io/freemagicspellsfx) by **The
    Soundrack**, [CC BY 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/): fire, ice, lightning,
    holy, acid, dark, wind, earth and water spells.
  - [RPG Sound Pack](https://opengameart.org/content/rpg-sound-pack) and
    [Swishes Sound Pack](https://opengameart.org/content/swishes-sound-pack) by **artisticdude**
    (CC0): sword unsheathing, swishes, slime and ogre sounds.
  - [Battle Sound Effects](https://opengameart.org/content/battle-sound-effects) by **Ogrebane**
    (CC0): swishes and a bow.
  - [80 CC0 RPG SFX](https://opengameart.org/content/80-cc0-rpg-sfx) by **rubberduck** (CC0):
    blade cuts, creature cries, coins, fire spells.
  - [Medieval sound effects - Weapon Textures](https://opengameart.org/content/medieval-sound-effects-weapon-textures)
    by **Still North Media** (Ben Jaszczak and Brian Nelson), CC0: axe, spear, katana, sabre,
    dagger and seax swings, longbow and recurve shots, crossbow shots and bolts, blade draws.
  - [3 Melee Sounds](https://opengameart.org/content/3-melee-sounds) (CC0): a bare-hand swish.
- Everything else (synths, drums, mixing, every song and arrangement) is made in code for this game.

Rebuild these files with `node tools/build-audio.mjs`.
